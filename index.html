Tetangga di luar 6D hanya menjaga kursi keenam bila ACTIVE keluarga independen, recurrence target-day, breadth runner-up, dan jeda kemunculannya mengungguli kursi lama. Pagar Target-Twin, near-parity, strong-win hedge, dual-route, replay-validated K, target-day, structural recovery, dan bridge lama tetap bekerja pada keadaan lain.
